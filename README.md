## Banco de México — Work & Projects

At Banxico's División de Asuntos Internacionales, I support analysis on international monetary policy and build tooling to make that analysis reproducible.

**IMF Article IV Recommendations to Mexico, 2015–2025**
An NLP pipeline that parses a decade of IMF Article IV consultations, extracts and classifies recommendations, and tracks them over time. Published as an interactive dashboard.
`Python` · `NLP` · `GitHub Pages` · `SVG data visualization`

**BCV Reincorporation into CEMLA — Policy Research Note**
A research note on the Banco Central de Venezuela's return to CEMLA, with editorial-style visualizations — political and sanctions timelines, an electoral risk calendar, and diplomatic relations tables — generated programmatically in Python.
`Python` · `data visualization` · `policy research`

**Institutional Reports Pipeline**
An internal automation pipeline for processing and structuring institutional reports at DAI, reducing manual review time.
`Python` · `NLP` · `automation`
